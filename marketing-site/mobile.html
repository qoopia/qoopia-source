Your Qoopia. On your iPhone.

Your dashboard, agents and memory in one iPhone app. Sign in with your Qoopia email and pick up where you left off.

The public beta is being prepared. Installation will open here after Apple approval.

  1. Get TestFlight

    Install Apple’s free TestFlight app. You only need to do this once. TestFlight ↗

  2. Install Qoopia

    Once the public beta opens, tap Install on iPhone here. TestFlight will open the Qoopia invitation. Tap Accept, then Install.

  3. Sign in and continue

    Open Qoopia, enter your usual email and confirm the email message. Return to the app — your linked dashboard opens automatically.

Qoopia for iPhone connects to your existing workspace. Its computer or server must stay online and be reachable from your phone. iOS 17 or later is required.

Free beta through Apple TestFlight. No App Store release yet.
