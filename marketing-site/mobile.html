Your Qoopia. On your iPhone.

Your dashboard, agents and memory in one iPhone app. Sign in with your Qoopia email and pick up where you left off.

The iPhone app opens a Qoopia server installation reachable over HTTPS. A Mac or Linux installation keeps its dashboard on that computer, listening only on its local address, so the app cannot open it yet.

Install on iPhone
  1. Get TestFlight

    Install Apple’s free TestFlight app. You only need to do this once. TestFlight ↗

  2. Install Qoopia

    Tap Install on iPhone above. TestFlight will open the Qoopia invitation. Tap Accept, then Install.

  3. Sign in and continue

    Open Qoopia and sign in with Google, or enter your usual email and open the link from the message on this iPhone. Your linked dashboard opens automatically.

Qoopia for iPhone opens a workspace on a server installation, which must stay online. A Mac or Linux installation keeps its dashboard on that computer: open it there. Your AI apps still reach its memory through the MCP address, which has no dashboard. iOS 17 or later is required.

Free beta through Apple TestFlight. No App Store release yet.
